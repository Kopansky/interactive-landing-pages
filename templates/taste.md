# Default client taste (start here when the client's own taste is unknown)

Distilled from ~100 pages a real client reviewed page by page. Copy it into the brief's **Client taste** list, then add or overrule items as this client reacts.

## Loves
- **Everything big:** huge headlines (hero ≥ 88px at 1440, often 120–150px), one idea per screen, big centred or edge-to-edge compositions.
- **A real concept:** the whole page follows one spine (a case file opening, a tree growing through life, a headset lit by its own light), and scroll drives it: pinned stages, things assembling, exploding, transforming, the camera travelling.
- **One strong visual system, used large:** flat geometric drawing (Figma-style), a bright isometric world, full-bleed cinematic photography, or a real 3D product. Not everything has to be drawn.
- **Single products** told like a product film: turn, take it apart, macro into a detail, colourways flooding the page, buy block with the same object (reference: the chef-knife, brewery-cans, keyboard and headset pages).
- **Clean:** few elements per screen, a frameless sticky header, generous space that still feels full.
- **Guessing well:** make smart assumptions, build, and list them. Don't ask more than the direction.

## Rejected
- Narrow text column with small type; a thin line or a few words on a dark page ("minimal is not premium").
- A stock photo in a small card or arch floating in white.
- Dashed placeholder boxes everywhere; placeholders must be small tags, at most one per section.
- Template or "local business" look; cloned sections; the same layout repeated down the page.
- Timid "calm editorial" with no concept; looking like a design tool.
- Framed or pill-shaped sticky headers.
- Unfinished work: anything shown must be finished, checked and consistent (a half-done page reads as bad).
