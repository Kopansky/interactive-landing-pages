# House styles — the quality bar

Contact sheets (12 frames, top to bottom of the page at 1440×900) of approved pages, and two rejected ones. The owner reviews everything shown to him and names only what he dislikes, so every page he didn't reject counts as approved. They are the visual bar for the art director's review (`references/art-director.md` §3): "is this page at the level of these?" Study how big, how full and how concept-led they are. **Never copy a concept or layout from them**; every client gets their own spine.

These are also the directions offered in the intake question (SKILL.md step 0).

| Style | Example sheet | What makes it work | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Real 3D product** (three.js, the object carries the story) | `good/keyboard-3d.jpg`, `good/headset-3d.jpg`, `good/smartlock-3d-hebrew.jpg` (Hebrew, RTL) | One object fills the screen; every beat changes camera and light (turn, explode into labelled parts, macro, colourways flooding the page, buy block with the same object) | `references/real-3d.md` |
| **Single-product film** (drawn or rendered product, no WebGL) | `good/knife-single-product.jpg` | "One knife." at huge size; the product crosses every screen: measured, taken apart, macro on the steel, into its box, price as a statement | `references/concepts.md`, `motion-recipes.md` |
| **Bold colour poster + photography** | `good/brewery-cans.jpg` | Each product floods the page in its own colour with a giant name behind it; poster type; photos full-bleed | `references/brand-styles.md` |
| **Cinematic photographic** (studio photo + scroll reveal) | `good/garage-xray-photo.jpg` | One white car in a studio; a scan line reveals a blueprint of each service's system; huge Hebrew headlines | `references/motion-recipes.md` §13 |
| **Flat drawn** (Figma-style illustration, big and bold) | `good/garage-flat-drawn.jpg`, `good/finance-drawn-tree.jpg` | One big drawn object that changes per chapter (bonnet opens, car lifts on a jack; a tree grows through life's seasons); full-screen colour per chapter | `references/motion-recipes.md` §15 |
| **Isometric world** | `good/finance-isometric-town.jpg`, `good/pharmacy-isometric.jpg` | A bright town the camera walks through; each stop builds as the camera arrives | `references/isometric.md` |
| **SaaS / app** (product UI as the hero) | `good/fitness-app-3d-phone.jpg` | A 3D phone whose live screens change with scroll; section colours wipe with the rhythm | `references/real-3d.md` (phones) |
