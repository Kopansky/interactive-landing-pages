# House styles — the quality bar

Contact sheets (12 frames, top to bottom of the page at 1440×900) of approved pages, and two rejected ones. The owner reviews every page and names only what they dislike, so every page not rejected counts as approved; these ~45 were also chosen by the art director as the strongest per style (pages with empty frames, small UI or pale screens were left out). They are the visual bar for the art director's review (`references/art-director.md` §3): "is this page at the level of these?" Study how big, how full and how concept-led they are. **Never copy a concept or layout from them**; every client gets their own spine.

The styles are also the directions offered in the intake question (SKILL.md step 0). All sheets are in `good/`.

| Style | Example sheets | What makes it work | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Real 3D product** (three.js, the object carries the story) | `keyboard-3d`, `headset-3d`, `watch-3d`, `smartlock-3d-hebrew` (Hebrew, RTL) | One object fills the screen; every beat changes camera and light (turn, explode into labelled parts, macro, colourways flooding the page, buy block with the same object) | `references/real-3d.md` |
| **Real 3D world** (three.js, services without a product — agencies, studios, consultancies) | `agency-3d-glass`, `agency-3d-clay-space`, `agency-3d-marble`, `agency-3d-inflatable`, `agency-3d-hologram`, `agency-3d-deepsea`, `agency-3d-bricks` | One invented world or machine carries every chapter (glass on an optical bench, a clay rocket flying planet to planet, a marble run, balloons); each service is a concrete built object with a sample UI on it; the real portfolio on screens held square-on with their captions; rich chapter colour, never a black void | `references/real-3d.md` |
| **Single-product film** (drawn, isometric or photo product, no WebGL) | `knife-single-product`, `espresso-isometric-product`, `ebike-photo-product`, `irrigation-photo-product` | The product at huge size crosses every screen: measured, taken apart, macro on a detail, in use, price as a statement | `references/concepts.md`, `motion-recipes.md` |
| **Bold colour poster** (flat colour per chapter, giant type, drawn or photo) | `brewery-cans`, `grill-colour-poster`, `icecream-drawn-poster`, `escape-room-drawn-poster`, `conference-kinetic-type`, `language-app-colour` | Each chapter floods the page in its own colour; one giant word or object per screen; poster type that fills the width | `references/brand-styles.md` |
| **Cinematic photographic** (full-bleed photo, scroll-driven reveal) | `garage-xray-photo`, `architect-cinematic-photo`, `car-brand-cinematic`, `law-photo`, `realestate-photo`, `vet-photo`, `drone-photo-lens`, `agency-photo-studio` (generated still life, backdrop recoloured per chapter) | Photos fill the screen; the camera moves through them (pull back, through a doorway, through a lens, scan line); huge headlines over light or dark | `references/motion-recipes.md` §13 |
| **Path** (a line the page follows) | `winery-photo-path`, `travel-globe-path`, `moving-drawn-road` | One line (a vine, a flight route, a road) runs down the page and connects every chapter | `references/concepts.md` |
| **Flat drawn** (Figma-style illustration, big and bold) | `garage-flat-drawn`, `finance-drawn-tree`, `music-school-drawn`, `trainer-drawn`, `yoga-drawn`, `handyman-drawn-xray`, `bookstore-popup-drawn` | One big drawn object or figure that changes per chapter (bonnet opens, tree grows, instrument per colour, pose per screen, a pop-up book opening); full-screen colour per chapter | `references/motion-recipes.md` §15 |
| **Isometric world** | `finance-isometric-town`, `pharmacy-isometric`, `festival-isometric-map`, `solar-isometric-house`, `realestate-isometric-city`, `camp-isometric-map`, `irrigation-isometric-garden` | A bright world the camera travels through; each stop builds or lights up as the camera arrives | `references/isometric.md` |
| **SaaS / app** (product UI as the hero) | `fitness-app-3d-phone`, `habit-app-dark`, `flights-app-cinematic`, `car-rental-app-dark`, `saas-monitoring-dark`, `savings-app-light`, `insurance-app-light` | The phone or dashboard is big and its screens change with scroll (a streak grows, a price line turns red, coins fill a jar); one strong accent | `references/real-3d.md` (phones), `brand-styles.md` |
